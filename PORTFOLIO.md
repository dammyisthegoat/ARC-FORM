# Portfolio notes — ARC/FORM

**Project type:** Premium accessories and personal technology storefront concept.

**Creative idea:** An editorial objects studio with obsidian surfaces, porcelain typography, a deliberate cobalt signal color, and restrained motion. The composition alternates cinematic imagery with technical index labels and material-led product details.

**Experience:** Responsive editorial homepage, 13-product catalog, working search/filter/sort controls, product variants and quantity controls, localStorage-backed shopping bag, and honest demo-only checkout/newsletter messaging.

**Stack:** HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript. No build step or paid dependency.
