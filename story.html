STUDIO NOTE / 001

01 THE BELIEF

A/FOBJECTS, CONSIDERED.

Not everything needs to be new.
It needs to be right.

ARC/FORM began with a simple question: what if the things we use every day were chosen with the same care as the things we keep for years?

We look for useful forms, honest materials and details that reward attention. Sometimes that means designing something from the ground up. Sometimes it means finding a maker who already got it right.

The result is a small, evolving collection of objects made to earn their place, not demand it.

02 OUR PRINCIPLES

Three things
we come back to.

01 / FUNCTION

Useful first.

An object earns its place by doing something well. We start with purpose, then refine until nothing feels unnecessary.

⌁
02 / MATERIAL

Honest to the touch.

Metal should feel like metal. Leather should tell its story. We pay attention to texture, finish, repair and the way materials change with time.

◌
03 / LONGEVITY

Made to stay.

We favour enduring forms over fast novelty. A good object should make sense today, and still feel right a few years from now.
